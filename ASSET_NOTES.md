## Collage

Final file: `assets/photos/anniversary-collage-baby.png` (1122 × 1402).

Nine distinct owner-supplied photos are arranged in a portrait collage. The first and ninth uploads were byte-identical, so that image appears once. The additional anniversary example supplies the design direction, not the people. Multi-pass compositing was required by the tool’s five-reference limit.

Include the nine supplied real portraits once each. Preserve their photographed faces, expressions, skin, hair, clothing, and detail; no fabricated portraits. Keep faces unobscured, crop the sideways event picture upright to the woman, and exclude the other person at its edge. Use restrained overlaps and generous safe margins. No dates, watermark, extra people, or device mockup.”

Final text-edit prompt: “Edit ONLY the small name beneath ‘happy anniversary’: replace ‘Shreya’ with the exact lowercase word ‘baby’, in the same elegant script size, color and position. Keep ‘happy anniversary’ exactly unchanged. Preserve every one of the nine photographs, all facial features and expressions, clothing, photo framing and positioning, all flowers, butterflies, background, borders and canvas dimensions pixel-faithfully. No other changes whatsoever.”

The caption is rendered separately from `content.finale.surprise.text`, so it can be edited without regenerating the collage.
